# tRNA 的生物功能与结构特征

**English title:** Biological functions and structural characteristics of tRNA

## 中文阐述
关注 tRNA 的经典三叶草二级结构、L 型三级结构、氨酰化、密码子解码、质量控制、tRNA 衍生片段以及疾病/应激中的功能重编程。

## English explanation
Focuses on tRNA secondary and tertiary structure, aminoacylation, codon decoding, quality control, tRNA-derived fragments, and disease/stress-associated functional rewiring.
