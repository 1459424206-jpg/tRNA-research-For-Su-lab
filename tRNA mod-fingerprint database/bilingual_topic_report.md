# tRNA 修饰指纹数据库

**English title:** tRNA mod-fingerprint database

## 中文阐述
关注 tRNA 修饰位点、修饰酶、LC-MS/MS 或测序产生的修饰指纹、跨物种数据库、标准化注释和可视化查询。

## English explanation
Focuses on tRNA modification sites, modifying enzymes, LC-MS/MS or sequencing-derived modification fingerprints, cross-species databases, standardized annotation, and visualization/query tools.
