### 8. Fig. 8 | Proposed mechanism of translational regulation mediated by desul-
Fig. 8 | Proposed mechanism of translational regulation mediated by desul- furized tRNA modiﬁcation. Under oxidative stress conditions, mcm5s2U34 mod- iﬁcation of cytoplasmic tRNAs for Lys, Gln, Glu, and Arg undergoes desulfuration, yielding mcm5h2U34. mcm5h2U34 decreases the aminoacylation efﬁciency of tRNALys, tRNAGln, and tRNAGlu, and also impairscodon recognition atthe A-site on the ribosome. As a result, codon-speciﬁc translation efﬁciency is negatively regulated.
